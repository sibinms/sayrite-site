FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Sayrite a free alternative to Wispr Flow?

Yes. Sayrite is free. Click the mic or hold a key, speak, and clean text appears in any app, with no account or subscription, and speech recognition can run entirely on your Mac.

Does Sayrite work offline?

Yes. With on-device speech recognition and basic editing, Sayrite works completely offline. Cloud speech and AI grammar correction are optional.

Which Macs does Sayrite support?

Macs with Apple silicon running macOS 13 or later. On macOS 26, Sayrite can use Apple’s built-in speech recognition with nothing extra to download.

Do I need an AI subscription?

No. Sayrite removes filler words and applies spoken corrections on its own. For AI grammar correction, connect your own Claude, OpenAI, or Groq account; Groq has a free plan.

Why does Sayrite ask for Input Monitoring and Accessibility?

Input Monitoring lets Sayrite notice your dictation shortcut and clicks on its mic. Accessibility lets it find the text box you’re in and type into it. It doesn’t record your keystrokes or read your screen.
